## Increasing demands of electricity for AI

   As we know, today web searches are gradually replaced by AI queries.
LLM has revolutionized code generation and has significant impact in jobs.


  Introduction of powerful LLM models have changed the web search patterns with LLM queries
increasing its presence in the searches. 
It also has negative impacts on website traffics. Here we will skip the discussion on that because
it is out of scope for this article.
